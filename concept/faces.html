How it would plug into the game

  • The player only picks three parts. A face is three small numbers (eyes, brows, mouth), sent once when you join a room. No art per combination.
  • The mood comes from the sim, every frame. KO event → KO'd. Holding charge while grounded → Charge. Airborne → Hop. Tilted past about 45% of the way to tipping → Wobble. Otherwise Idle.
  • Every part draws every mood. So a KO face keeps your style: googly eyes become spirals, shades crack, a gold grill loses a tooth.
  • It's all vector paths. The same drawing code can run on the game canvas (Path2D), or be exported to PNG sprites.
